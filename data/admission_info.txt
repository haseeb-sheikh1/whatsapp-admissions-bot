INSTITUTION NAME: Excellence Institute of Higher Education

PROGRAMS OFFERED:
- BS Computer Science (4 years)
- BS Business Administration (4 years)  
- BS Engineering (4 years)
- MBA (2 years)
- MBBS (5 years)
- BBA (4 years)

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Matric (SSC): Minimum 60% marks required
- Inter (HSSC / FSc): Minimum 55% marks required
- No age restriction
- Both pre-medical and pre-engineering students eligible

ADMISSION PROCESS:
1. Submit application online or in person
2. Provide attested documents (Matric, Inter certificates, CNIC)
3. Attend entrance test (if required for your program)
4. Merit list announced within 2 weeks of closing date
5. Pay fee upon selection

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS:
Q: Is there an entrance test?
A: Yes, for MBBS and Engineering. BS CS, BBA, MBA do not require an entrance test.

Q: Can I apply if I have below 55% in FSc?
A: Unfortunately, we require a minimum of 55% in FSc for eligibility. However, you may apply for our diploma programs with lower marks.

Q: Is hostel available?
A: Yes, both boys and girls hostels are available on campus. Monthly hostel fee is PKR 8,000 (including meals).

Q: When will classes start?
A: For the upcoming intake, classes begin February 3, 2025.

Q: Is the degree recognized?
A: Yes, our institution is recognized by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan.
